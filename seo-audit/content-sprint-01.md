## Quality Requirements

Every article must include:

- Original formulas and worked examples checked independently.
- Illustrative data labels wherever real customer data is not used.
- One clear H1 and descriptive H2 hierarchy.
- A concise direct answer near the top.
- At least five useful internal links.
- A calculator or relevant tool CTA.
- A demo/WhatsApp CTA only after useful information is delivered.
- Article, BreadcrumbList, and eligible FAQ structured data.
- An accurate title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata, and image alt text.
- No unsupported performance guarantees, veterinary claims, customer numbers, or invented quotations.

## LinkedIn Launch Sequence

### Post 1: The Cost Visibility Problem

Opening idea: Purchase price and selling price do not reveal the true profit of an animal.

Core points: Feed, medicine, labour, mortality, days on feed, and weight gain determine the margin. Link to the cattle profit calculator.

### Post 2: Calculator Walkthrough

Show the inputs, result categories, cost per kg gained, and break-even selling price. State that results are planning estimates, not guarantees.

### Post 3: Animal-Level Economics

Explain why herd averages can hide underperforming animals and why purchase-to-sale records matter.

### Post 4: Feed Cost And ADG

Use one illustrative scenario to show how a small change in daily feed cost or ADG changes projected margin.

### Post 5: Product Workflow

Use the workflow: Purchase -> Feed -> Weight -> Health -> Cost -> Sale -> Profit. Link to the cattle fattening software page and demo.
