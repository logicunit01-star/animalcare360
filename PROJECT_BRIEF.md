# AnimalCare360 Website Reinvention: Master Instruction

**Decision date:** 4 October 2026  
**Status:** Approved strategic direction and implementation authority  
**Primary website:** https://www.animalcare360.com  

## Why We Are Reinventing The Website

AnimalCare360 has a broad and capable product ecosystem, but the website currently asks visitors to understand four different businesses at the same time:

1. Livestock and cattle farm management
2. Feed and Wanda retail
3. Animal trading
4. Pet hospital and veterinary clinic management

This communicates product breadth but weakens commercial relevance. A cattle operator, feed retailer, animal trader and veterinary clinic do not share the same urgent problem, vocabulary, buying process or proof requirements.

The website is being reinvented to give AnimalCare360 one memorable commercial entry point while preserving the broader product portfolio.

## Final Strategic Decision

AnimalCare360 will be a **global vertical SaaS business from the start**, focused by operational niche rather than geography.

The primary acquisition wedge for the next six months is:

> **Commercial Cattle Fattening, Beef Finishing and Feedlot Management**

The central promise is:

> **Know the complete cost, weight performance and profit of every animal.**

The core operating story is:

```text
Purchase
  → Animal Registration
  → Feed
  → Weight
  → Health
  → Expenses
  → Cost
  → Sale
  → Profit
```

Use “true cost” only where the product can distinguish measured costs from allocated shared costs. Do not overstate individual feed measurement when feed is recorded by pen or batch.

## Portfolio Rule

AnimalCare360 remains a four-product business. It will not market all four products equally on the homepage.

- **Primary acquisition product:** CattlePro and cattle economics
- **Secondary solution:** Feed and animal-feed retail POS
- **Secondary solution:** Animal trading and deal profitability
- **Secondary solution:** Pet hospital and veterinary clinic ERP

Existing pages remain accessible and indexable. They move into a clear product/solutions hierarchy and must not compete with the homepage's primary cattle-economics message.

Recommended six-month marketing allocation:

| Product line | Share of acquisition effort |
|---|---:|
| CattlePro / cattle fattening | 70% |
| Feed retail | 12% |
| Animal trading | 10% |
| Veterinary and pet hospital | 8% |

These percentages guide focus; they are not permanent budget commitments.

## Global-From-Start Requirements

- Use international English and market-neutral canonical URLs.
- Recognize cattle fattening, beef finishing, feedlot, backgrounding and cost-of-gain terminology.
- Support selectable currency and metric/imperial units in the calculator.
- Do not force country redirects or make Pakistan the brand identity.
- Offer email and calendar-based contact globally; use WhatsApp where appropriate.
- Add country/language pages only when pricing, terminology, support, proof and legal content are genuinely localized.
- Do not publish a founder's home address, private office address or precise operating location.
- Use lawful registered-office details only where legally required for contracts, billing, regulatory notices or payment providers.
- Never publish a customer's precise farm address. Country or broad region requires customer approval.

## Evidence Baseline

GA4 property reviewed on 4 October 2026 for 6 September–3 October 2026:

- 60 active users
- 53 new users
- 97 sessions
- 136 page views
- 35 engaged sessions
- 36.08% engagement rate
- 42 seconds average engagement time per active user
- 0 key events
- 0 recorded revenue

Acquisition sessions included direct (39), Google organic (37), ChatGPT (9), Bing organic (4), Vercel referral (4) and Perplexity (1).

Pakistan was the largest single country with 22 active users, but approximately 63% of users were outside Pakistan. The sample is too small to prove market demand or support a conventional statistical A/B test.

The homepage recorded 65 views, 27 active users and a 72.9% bounce rate. CattlePro and pricing each received seven views. The data does not justify equal promotion of four domains.

Only basic GA4 events were arriving: `page_view`, `session_start`, `first_visit`, `user_engagement`, `scroll` and `click`. Source-defined demo, trial, pricing and WhatsApp events were not arriving as GA4 events. Conversion instrumentation must be repaired before marketing performance is judged.

## Primary Conversion Funnel

```text
Google / AI search / YouTube / LinkedIn / Referral
  → Commercial page or useful content
  → Cattle Fattening Profit Calculator
  → Immediate result with visible assumptions
  → Lead or preferred contact channel
  → Qualified demo
  → Guided trial and onboarding
  → Activated farm
  → Paid customer
```

The calculator must provide useful results before asking for contact information. It is a planning tool, not a veterinary or financial guarantee.

## Website Outcome

The first viewport must answer:

1. Who is this for?
2. What costly problem does it solve?
3. What operational or financial result does it provide?
4. Why is AnimalCare360 credible?
5. What should the visitor do next?

Primary homepage message:

> **Know the Cost, Weight Performance and Profit of Every Animal.**

Primary CTA:

> **Calculate Your Cattle Profit**

Secondary CTA:

> **Book a Demo**

The homepage must not become a catalogue. Product capabilities should be organized around animal costing, feed management, weight and performance, health, inventory, profitability, multi-farm operations and partner/Palai workflows.

## Initial Implementation Scope

The approved coordinated revamp includes:

1. Homepage positioning and conversion architecture
2. Navigation and portfolio hierarchy
3. `/cattle-fattening-software`
4. `/cattle-fattening-profit-calculator`
5. `/demo`
6. `/customers` architecture without fabricated stories
7. Cattle-management and CattlePro intent clarification
8. Global metadata, social previews, schema and internal links
9. GA4/GTM event delivery and funnel measurement
10. Mobile, accessibility, performance and regression verification

Existing indexed URLs must not be deleted. New content routes enter the sitemap only when complete. Pricing changes require separate approval.

## Meaning Of “Complete In One Go”

Implement the approved core revamp as one coordinated local/staging change set so design, copy, SEO, analytics and responsive behavior work together. Do not release a half-repositioned homepage with missing calculator, broken events or inconsistent navigation.

“One go” does not mean:

- blindly deploying to production;
- creating every future calculator and regional page;
- publishing thin AI articles;
- changing backend APIs, authentication or databases;
- changing production prices without approval;
- claiming unverified product behavior or customer outcomes.

The release still passes build, lint, route, metadata, structured-data, analytics, responsive, accessibility and visual QA before deployment.

## 90-Day Validation Targets

- Complete and verify the core website revamp.
- Repair conversion tracking and establish a clean baseline.
- Conduct 15–20 interviews with qualified global cattle operators.
- Recruit up to 5 design-partner or guided-trial farms.
- Publish one commercial landing page, one useful calculator and four substantial economic guides.
- Obtain at least one approved customer story or evidence-backed workflow case.
- Measure qualified leads, calculator completion, demos, trial activation and willingness to pay.

## Six-Month Commercial Targets

- 20–30 qualified cattle-operation demos.
- 5–10 paying cattle/fattening operations.
- At least 3 farms consistently entering core operational data.
- 2 verified customer stories.
- One repeatable acquisition channel.
- Evidence for or against the cattle-fattening wedge based on paid conversion and retention, not pageviews alone.

## 2027 Planning Model

This is an internal planning framework, not a guarantee:

- Base case: 40–70 paying farms/businesses
- Approximate realized ARPA reference: PKR 12,000–18,000 or market-equivalent pricing
- Approximate late-2027 MRR reference: PKR 500k–1.2M
- Upside case: 80–120+ customers and PKR 1.2M–2M+ MRR equivalent

Global reporting should use a consistent base currency and track regional revenue, exchange rates, onboarding cost, support load, churn and collection timing.

## Success Metrics

**Acquisition:** qualified non-brand traffic, commercial keyword visibility, calculator entrances and direct leads.  
**Engagement:** calculator completion, result CTA engagement, pricing engagement and demo/contact requests.  
**Sales:** lead quality, demo-to-trial, trial activation, trial-to-paid, CAC, realized ARPA, MRR and churn.  
**Customer success:** activated farms, active animals managed, core workflow completion, weekly users and retention.

Pageviews, impressions, keyword count and article count are diagnostic metrics, not the business objective.

## Non-Negotiable Guardrails

- Do not fabricate customers, testimonials, ratings, adoption numbers or financial outcomes.
- Label sample and illustrative data clearly.
- Do not make unsupported veterinary, financial, tax, legal or AI diagnostic claims.
- Do not expose personal or precise location information.
- Do not remove indexed pages without an approved redirect and migration plan.
- Do not modify APIs, databases, authentication or SaaS application behavior as part of the marketing-site revamp.
- Do not hard-code tracking IDs when environment configuration is appropriate.
- Do not send personal data, animal IDs or exact financial scenarios to analytics.
- Prefer real product screenshots and verified workflows over generic marketing imagery.

## Decision Rule

Stay focused on commercial cattle fattening for six months while keeping the broader portfolio intact. Reassess using qualified pipeline, activated trials, paid farms, retention and realized ARPA. Do not abandon or expand the niche based on a small traffic sample, a single campaign or vanity metrics.
